- **DNS is now watched too.** The dropdown shows the resolvers macOS actually uses
  and whether they're encrypted, and a lightweight probe checks that your queries
  really leave through the tunnel — with a ⚠️ badge when they don't.
- **The IPv6 exit is always shown** when it can be measured, so a v4-only tunnel
  can't quietly leak your v6 traffic past it.
- **The active route names itself**: Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or iPhone USB, read from the
  system rather than guessed.
- **Add to Applications folder** puts WhereAmIP in Spotlight and Launchpad without
  moving anything — one toggle in Settings, reversible any time.
- This window can be reopened whenever you want, from **Settings ▸ Show Welcome
  Window**.
